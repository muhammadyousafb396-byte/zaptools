About YouTube thumbnails

Every public YouTube video has auto-generated thumbnails at several resolutions, served from YouTube's image CDN. This tool only displays those public images — it never downloads or copies video content, which keeps it fully within policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I download the thumbnails?

Click 'Open' on any quality to view full-size, then save from your browser.

Why is maxres missing sometimes?

YouTube only generates maxres for videos uploaded in HD+; older videos top out at lower qualities.

Does this download videos?

No — thumbnails only. We never touch video streams.
