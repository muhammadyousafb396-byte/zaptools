How to calculate your exact age

Select your date of birth and click Calculate Age. The tool computes your completed years, remaining months and days — plus your total days lived and a countdown to your next birthday.

What "exact age" means

Your chronological age is the full years elapsed since birth. Our calculator also breaks the remainder into months and days, so "32 years, 4 months, 12 days" is precise as of today.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated exactly?

By comparing your birth date with today's date: full years first, then remaining months and days, accounting for different month lengths and leap years.

Can I calculate age for a future date?

No — the birth date must be today or earlier. For days between any two dates, use our Date Duration Calculator.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
