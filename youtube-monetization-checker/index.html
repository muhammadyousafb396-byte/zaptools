YouTube Partner Program requirements (2026)

To monetize with ads you need 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months or 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days. You must also follow all YouTube monetization policies, live in an eligible country and have an AdSense account.

Lower fan-funding tier

500 subscribers + 3,000 watch hours (or 3M Shorts views) unlocks channel memberships, Super Chat and Shopping — but not ad revenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does YPP review take?

Typically a few days to 2–4 weeks. YouTube reviews manually in order received.

Why do channels get rejected?

Reused content (compilations without commentary), repetitious content, artificial traffic and policy violations are the top reasons.

Do Shorts views count for watch hours?

No — Shorts feed views don't count toward the 4,000 hours, but they do count toward the 10M Shorts path.

Can I apply with 900 subscribers?

No — thresholds are hard minimums. Keep publishing until you cross them.
