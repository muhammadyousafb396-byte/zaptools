How it works

Your browser has built-in speech voices — no audio files are generated on a server. Pick a voice, adjust speed and pitch, and listen instantly. Great for proofreading, accessibility and language learning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which voices are available?

Voices come from your operating system and browser — Windows, macOS, Android and Chrome each include several.

Can I download the audio?

Browser speech can't be saved directly here; use a screen recorder if you need an MP3.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the page loads — voices are built into your device.
