Frequently Asked Questions

What assumptions are used?

40 hours/week, 52 weeks/year (2,080 hours). Part-time or overtime needs custom math.

Is tax included?

No — these are gross figures. Net pay depends on your tax bracket and location.

How do I compare freelance rates?

Enter your hourly freelance rate and see the annualized equivalent — then add ~25–30% for benefits you'd get as an employee.
