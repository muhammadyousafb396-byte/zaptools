What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening measure that estimates body fat from height and weight: BMI = weight(kg) ÷ height(m)².

BMI categories (WHO)

  • Below 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9 — Normal weight
  • 25 to 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30 and above — Obese

Note: BMI is a general guide and doesn't distinguish muscle from fat. Athletes and pregnant women should consult a doctor.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered the healthy range for most adults.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Not always — muscle weighs more than fat, so muscular people may show a high BMI while being healthy.
