Frequently Asked Questions

How long should my GIF be?

2–6 seconds is ideal. Longer clips make huge files — trim to the best moment.

What FPS should I choose?

10–12 FPS keeps files small and smooth enough. 15+ looks better but grows the file fast.

Is there a watermark?

No — GIFs are clean, with no watermark, ever.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion runs locally with FFmpeg in your browser.
