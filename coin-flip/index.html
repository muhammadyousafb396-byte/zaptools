Frequently Asked Questions

Yes — each flip is independent with exactly equal odds.

What are the stats for?

They show your session's heads/tails counts so you can see randomness evening out over many flips.

Can I use it for giveaways?

For casual decisions yes; for prizes, our Random Number Generator with no-repeats is better.
