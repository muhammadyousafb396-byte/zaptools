Why a world clock?

Remote teams, international calls and travel planning all need quick time-zone checks. Times update live using your device clock and official IANA time-zone data — including daylight saving.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are daylight saving changes handled?

Yes — times use official time-zone databases that include DST rules.

Can I add more cities?

This view covers major hubs. Your browser's clock provides your local reference at the bottom.

Is the time exact?

It matches your device clock, which is typically synced within a second.
