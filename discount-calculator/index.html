How discounts work

Sale price = original price × (1 − discount%). A 25% discount on $100 saves you $25 — you pay $75. Stack multiple discounts by applying them one after another, not by adding percentages.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 30% off $80?

$80 × 0.70 = $56. You save $24.

Do two 20% discounts equal 40% off?

No — sequential discounts multiply: 20% then 20% = 36% off total.

Does sales tax apply before or after discount?

After — tax is calculated on the discounted price in most places.
