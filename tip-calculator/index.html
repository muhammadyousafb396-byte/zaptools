Frequently Asked Questions

What is a standard tip at restaurants?

18–20% of the pre-tax bill is standard in the US; 10–15% in many other countries.

Should I tip on tax?

Etiquette says tip on the pre-tax subtotal.

Can I split unevenly?

This tool splits evenly. For uneven splits, calculate each person's share separately.
