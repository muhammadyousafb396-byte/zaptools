How to use the scientific calculator

Click the buttons or type directly with your keyboard. Trigonometric functions use radians. Press = or Enter to evaluate the full expression shown on the display.

Supported functions

  • Trigonometry: sin, cos, tan and their inverses (asin, acos, atan)
  • Logarithms: log (base 10) and ln (natural log)
  • Powers & roots: x², x³, xʸ, √ square root
  • Constants: π (pi) and e (Euler's number)
  • More: factorial (n!), reciprocal (1/x), absolute value, percentage

FAQs

Is this scientific calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no signup and no limits.

Can I use my keyboard?

Yes. Type digits, + - * / ( ) . and press Enter for equals, Backspace to delete, Escape to clear.

Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes, the calculator is fully responsive and touch-friendly.
