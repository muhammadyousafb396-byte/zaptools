Rank higher on YouTube

YouTube is the world's second-biggest search engine. The right title, tags, description and hashtags help the algorithm understand your video and show it to the right viewers.

Quick wins

Put your main keyword in the first 40 characters of the title, write 150+ word descriptions with timestamps, and use 3–5 hashtags. Then make the video deliver on the title's promise — watch time beats everything.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many tags should a YouTube video have?

YouTube allows 500 characters of tags. Use 10–15 focused tags: your main keyword first, then variations and related terms.

What makes a good YouTube title?

Under 60 characters, keyword near the front, a curiosity hook or number, and no clickbait that the video doesn't deliver.

Do hashtags help on YouTube?

The first 3 hashtags show above your title and aid discovery. Use 3–5 relevant ones; stuffing 15+ looks spammy.

Should keywords go in the description?

Yes — the first 2–3 lines matter most (they show in search). Repeat your main keyword naturally 2–3 times in the full description.
