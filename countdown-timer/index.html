Frequently Asked Questions

Will it beep when finished?

Yes — three audible beeps play when the countdown reaches zero.

Can I pause and resume?

Yes, the Start button toggles to Pause/Resume.

Does it work in a background tab?

Mostly yes, though some browsers throttle background timers slightly.
