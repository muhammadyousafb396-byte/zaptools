What are hashes for?

  • Integrity: verify downloads by comparing checksums
  • Passwords: stored as hashes, never plaintext
  • Git & blockchains: content addressing

SHA-256 is the modern standard; SHA-1 is legacy and shouldn't secure anything new.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can hashes be reversed?

No — cryptographic hashes are one-way by design.

Why do two similar texts give totally different hashes?

The avalanche effect: one changed character flips ~half the output bits.

Is hashing the same as encryption?

No. Encryption is reversible with a key; hashing is one-way.
