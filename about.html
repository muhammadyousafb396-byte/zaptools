About ZapTools

Free online tools, built privacy-first.

Our story

ZapTools started in 2026 with a simple frustration: every "free" online tool seemed to demand a signup, watermark your output, or upload your files to a server you don't control. We believed everyday utilities — converting a currency, counting words, extracting text from a screenshot — shouldn't cost privacy or patience.

So we built ZapTools: a collection of 50 free online tools that run entirely in your browser. No accounts. No watermarks. No file uploads. Your images, videos and documents never leave your device.

What we offer

  • Finance tools — currency converter with live rates, loan/EMI, tip, discount and compound interest calculators
  • Everyday calculators — scientific calculator, age, BMI, percentage, GPA, salary and fuel cost calculators
  • Text utilities — word counter, case converter, fancy text, text-to-speech, JSON formatter, Base64 and more
  • Image & video tools — OCR, image converter, text-to-image maker, video-to-audio extractor, QR generator
  • Developer & SEO tools — hash generator, URL encoder, website SEO checker, YouTube thumbnail viewer
  • Time & fun — stopwatch, countdown timer, world clock, dice roller, coin flip, random numbers

Our privacy philosophy

Most tools on ZapTools use pure client-side JavaScript — including heavy tasks like OCR (Tesseract.js) and video-to-audio conversion (FFmpeg WebAssembly). The only network requests are to free public APIs (currency rates) and CDNs hosting open-source libraries. We don't operate user accounts, so there's nothing to leak.

How we stay free

ZapTools is supported by advertising. We show a small number of unobtrusive ads so all 50 tools can remain free forever. We never sell data — we barely collect any.
