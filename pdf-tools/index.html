Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a page or size limit for merging?

Browser memory is the limit — merging a dozen typical PDFs works smoothly.

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. All PDF processing happens locally in your browser.

Can I reorder pages before merging?

Files merge in the order selected. To reorder, select them in your desired order.

Does text extraction keep formatting?

Plain text is extracted in reading order; complex layouts may lose columns and tables.
