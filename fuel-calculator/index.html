Frequently Asked Questions

What is good fuel economy in 2026?

30+ MPG is solid for gas cars; hybrids reach 50+; EVs use MPGe instead.

How do I convert MPG to L/100km?

L/100km = 235.214 ÷ MPG. Lower L/100km is better.

Does AC really use that much fuel?

AC can cut economy 5–25% in stop-and-go traffic; at highway speeds its effect is smaller than open windows' drag.
