Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my notes stored?

In your browser's localStorage on this device only.

Will I lose notes if I clear browser data?

Yes — clearing site data erases them. Download .txt backups for anything important.

Can I access notes on another device?

No — that's the privacy tradeoff. Use download/upload to move them.
