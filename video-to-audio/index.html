How to extract audio from video

  1. Upload your video file above.
  2. Choose MP3 quality and click Extract Audio.
  3. Preview and download the MP3.

Privacy note: conversion uses FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly running locally. The ~25MB engine downloads once, then everything happens on your device — no server ever sees your video.

FAQs

Is video to audio conversion free?

Yes, completely free with no limits on file count.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion runs in your browser with FFmpeg WebAssembly — your video never leaves your device.

Why does the first conversion take a while?

The FFmpeg engine (~25MB) downloads once on first use and is then cached by your browser.
