Frequently Asked Questions

How many miles is 1 kilometer?

1 kilometer equals 0.621371 miles.

What is 0°C in Fahrenheit?

0°C equals 32°F. Our converter handles all temperature math including Kelvin.

Does it work offline?

Yes — after the page loads, all conversions run locally without internet.
