What can QR codes hold?

URLs, plain text, email addresses, phone numbers and WiFi credentials. Phones scan them natively with the camera app — no special reader needed.

Uses

  • Link packaging, menus and business cards to your website
  • Share WiFi passwords with guests
  • Event tickets and contact cards (vCards)

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the QR codes free for commercial use?

Yes — unlimited, no watermark, yours to use anywhere.

Do QR codes expire?

No. A static QR code works forever as long as the encoded link stays live.

What size should I download?

300px works for screens; use 500px+ for print. Always test-scan before printing.
