Color harmony basics

  • Analogous: neighbors on the color wheel — calm, cohesive
  • Complementary: opposites — bold contrast
  • Monochrome: one hue, many lightnesses — elegant minimalism

Frequently Asked Questions

How are palettes generated?

Hue rotations around the color wheel with tuned saturation/lightness per style.

Can I lock a color I like?

Regenerate until you like the set, then copy the HEX values you want to keep.

Are palettes accessible?

Check contrast ratios for text use — pretty palettes aren't always readable.
