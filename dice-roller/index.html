Frequently Asked Questions

What does 2d6+3 mean?

Roll two 6-sided dice, add them, then add 3.

Is the roll fair?

Yes — cryptographic randomness gives every face equal probability.

Can I roll 20 dice at once?

Up to 20 dice per roll, enough for fireball damage.
